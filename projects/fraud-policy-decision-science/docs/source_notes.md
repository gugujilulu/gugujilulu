## Reconciliation decisions

Frequency growth of 49% and severity growth of 12% imply approximately 66.9% growth in loss amount within an identical cohort; they do not independently reconstruct the 0.44% to 0.78% rate change. Complete amount denominators and cohort definitions are required for an exact bridge.

Category and merchant contributions overlap. The 1,000-per-arm selection example is a separate teaching construction from the approximately 80,000-transaction experiment plan.

The operations table gives incremental net values of $740K for capacity, $630K for automation and $1.09M for their combination. It supports the combination outperforming either component; superadditivity requires a matched factorial comparison and is not established by these totals.

For the adaptive pilot, $310K in net-loss savings less $95K in lost margin and $40K in authentication cost gives $175K before other operating costs. Adding the $70K recovery spillover requires confirming that it is excluded from the $310K. The headline avoids an unresolved sum.

No confidence intervals, p-values or fitted policy-value estimates are reconstructed from aggregate point estimates alone.
